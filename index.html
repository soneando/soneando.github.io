All of Me Club六本木 / Roppongi
Jazz Bar

A jazz bar with thirty years in Roppongi — grand piano, a long bar, food by a chef who trained nineteen years in Bologna. Most nights it's jazz. One Wednesday a month, it's Cuba.

Cafe La Vida広尾 / Hiroo
Cuban Restaurant
広尾駅から3分、階段を降りた先のキューバ料理店。黒豆ごはん、揚げバナナ、じっくり焼いた豚に冷えたモヒート。まず食事がおいしい店です。食べて飲んでいるうちにバンドが始まって、気づけば踊っている人がいます。
A Cuban restaurant down a flight of stairs, three minutes from Hiroo station. Black bean rice, fried plantains, slow-roasted pork, cold mojitos — come hungry. The band starts somewhere between dinner and the next round, and people end up dancing.
